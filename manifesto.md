# Manifesto

Scripture has never lacked truth.

It has often lacked attention.

We live in a world where billions of people spend hours each day consuming stories that are forgotten tomorrow.

We believe the greatest story ever told deserves the same level of craftsmanship, beauty, and innovation as the world's best entertainment—not to replace God's Word, but to help people encounter it.

Every animation. Every line of code. Every frame. Every sound. Every interaction.

Exists for one purpose:

**To make Scripture impossible to ignore.**
