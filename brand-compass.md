# Brand Compass

## Mission
To make Scripture impossible to ignore.

## Vision
To create the world's most beautiful, engaging, and biblically faithful way to experience God's Word.

## Promise
One swipe closer to Scripture.

## Standard
Scripture first. Spectacle second.

## Product Philosophy
Build something so beautiful that people stop scrolling, and so faithful that they start reading.

## Success
A user closes the experience wanting to open their Bible.
