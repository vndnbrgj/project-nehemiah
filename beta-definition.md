# Beta Definition

The beta is complete when a new user can open the product, swipe through Scripture-centered content, read the related passage, save content, and leave wanting to open their Bible.

## Included
- Vertical swipe feed
- Verse experiences
- Bible story videos
- Daily featured verse
- Anonymous browsing
- Optional account
- Favorites
- Continue watching
- Related passage and context
- Clearly displayed translation

## Initial Content Target
- 100 verse experiences
- 25 story episodes
- 5 topical collections
- 20 guided prayers

## Excluded
AI chat, comments, ads, subscriptions, church dashboards, creator accounts, and complex social features.
