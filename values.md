# Company Values

## Scripture Above Everything
The Bible is the authority.

## Scripture First. Spectacle Second.
Visual quality must serve biblical faithfulness.

## Excellence Honors God
We pursue thoughtful craftsmanship.

## Wonder Leads to Curiosity
Beauty should invite deeper exploration.

## Build for Skeptics
The experience should welcome people unfamiliar with the Bible.

## Leave Them Wanting More
The product succeeds when people want to keep reading Scripture.

## Stewardship
We spend carefully and avoid paying to solve problems we do not yet have.
